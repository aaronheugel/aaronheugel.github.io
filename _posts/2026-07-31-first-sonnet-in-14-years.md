title: First Sonnet in 14 Years
date: 2026-07-31 14:00
---

Today I choose to start a new project\
Requiring my thought and time such that\
My mind does not wander to the hot mess\
Of missing out on where I know the path\
Was laid for me to learn the most of this\
A life beholden to the rules of death\
A heart unbridled, pardon my wishes\
Enthusiasm and all the good health\
Its not something I ought to know about\
If I wanted to start over again\
Might be something I ought to be about\
If I wanted to see my heart again\
The moon still my never forgotten friend\
The sun, shining til my unbitter end.
