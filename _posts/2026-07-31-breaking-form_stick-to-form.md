title: Breaking Form/Stick to Form
date: 2026-07-31 17:00

If I have enabled this light go on\
I’m sorry— traumatized with guilt I move\
Away from what I did before, bygone\
Be bygone I’m a new man alive of\
Better character than a day ago\
Unresistant to the morning or bed\
Moving along entirely new flows\
Hippie and professional ahead\
Of the game, no longer is it a game\
Form is a substrate for my successes\
I’m disastrous if I’m not the same\
As I was once, confused, but relaxing\
Basking in the beauty of poetry\
Not eyeing the price of my novelty
