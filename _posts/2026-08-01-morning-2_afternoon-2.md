A poem a day is not a comfort\
More like a notice I cannot live to\
Up on my low bar, just about done for\
I did it before but now it feels new\
This skill I’ve deemed talent now to be trained\
Is not a comparison of my past\
The heart is a funny drug, often said\
Its warmth, the rust, I say it does not last\
If you get after it, morning again\
Morning again, go after it, take pride\
In dedication to fun and the pen\
Let it be what you want to be, the vibe\
Of rigorous good life from here on out\
Confusion distilled, its poetry now
