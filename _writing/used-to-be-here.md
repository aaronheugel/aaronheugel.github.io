---
title: Used to be here
---

Fortune would have it, I say\
As I reach into my back pocket\
To pull out a folded piece of paper\
This is the piece, I intonate\
As I pull a lighter from my front pocket\
With my other hand, and I spark it\
Flame to paper and it takes\
Engulfed like my new dreams

I am not the victim or the benefactor\
Or even a bystander, the crime\
Is not related to me

I’m just a patron of the arts, get off me
