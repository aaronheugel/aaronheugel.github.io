---
title: For Andrea
---

I’m not the one you’d wished I’d be, I get it\
I hope you understood that I do know\
I’ll never understand my privilege\
But I’m trying, and I’m trying to know I’m trying\
Every day I remember to\
But I have to say, you\
You were the one that convinced me to give it a shot\
That you told me\
It’s not where you come from but what you write down\
And are willing to say\
To a real audience willing to hear me out\
So hear me out

Confusion is beautiful\
I’ve learned, if you keep looking\
For answers real answers\
And bow to confidence knowing it is separate\
From myself
